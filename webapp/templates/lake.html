Baseline = mean of the earlier optical values for this calendar month; score = (area − baseline) ÷ their standard deviation. A score needs 5 earlier values from 2 years. It is a statistical deviation, not a danger level or a probability.

Radar (Sentinel-1)

Radar is measured and shown, but not risk-scored in this build. In summer it agrees with near-same-day optical to within about 5–6% (Karambar +5.2%, Atro Sar −6.4%; 16 summer scenes, 2 lakes), but during snowmelt (April to June) wet snow looks like water on radar, and those errors produced the largest false anomalies. Scoring stays off until a melt-season check has been tested.
