Honest limits

  • Screening, not forecasting. Weights and thresholds are judgement calls, not calibrated on past events. No field validation.
  • Clouds and snow: lake areas need clear, snow-free days, so the record is mostly June–October.
  • Resolution: 10 m Sentinel-2, 30 m DEM and Hansen, 100 m GHSL. Sites under {{ d.settings.min_patch }} ha are not reported.
  • Vegetation loss ≠ deforestation: the cause is not known. Only {% if d.forest.agreement.ours_confirmed_by_hansen is not none %}{{ "%.1f" | format(d.forest.agreement.ours_confirmed_by_hansen * 100) }} %{% else %}–{% endif %} of our loss is also in Hansen.
  • Downhill path is one line, not a flood model: no depth, volume or timing.
  • Lakes are unnamed in OpenStreetMap; village names and Urdu spellings need local checking.
  • Urdu/Pashto text and voices are machine-generated; the Pashto voice is an Afghan voice and may not match Swat speech.
  • Glacier layer reused from our final-year project (SailAb GLOF).
  • Nothing is sent. "Ready" is only a label.
