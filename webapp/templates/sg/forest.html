What the numbers mean

Hansen (30 m, yearly maps) finds very little recent loss in this valley. Our 10 m NDVI comparison finds much more, and it is not all tree cutting: a drop in NDVI also comes from debris flows, river-bank erosion, construction, crops or a dry summer. So ShinGhar calls it vegetation loss in forest areas and does not claim a cause. Hansen's own "tree cover loss" also includes fire, landslides and disease; neither is the same as deforestation.
